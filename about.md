**Swartzism** is a doctrine of information power, built from the record of Aaron Swartz — his essays, his manifestos, his projects, and his failures. It holds that:

- Knowledge about the world is a commons, and its enclosure is a harm.
- Information is power, and power should be accountable.
- Data about persons is not a commons. Privacy is a power too, held by the powerless against the powerful.
- A doctrine that needs its subject to be flawless is a hagiography, not an argument.

The name is a construction, not a claim. Swartz did not name a movement; he left a record. This blog argues about what that record implies — and stress-tests the argument.

Part of the **CCAgentOrg** open-data ecosystem. Analysis by [CashlessConsumer](https://cashlessconsumer.in), a fintech & DPI researcher.
